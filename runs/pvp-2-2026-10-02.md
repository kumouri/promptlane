## 0. What was built, at $0

### 0.1 The package, and what was deferred

| piece (Margo's summary of the approval) | built as | status |

| walking, killable couriers | — | **deferred** (§4) |

How pvp-2 is played: `--map pvp-2 --resolution simultaneous-1 --recall recall-2`. A scaled map needs the
simultaneous resolution, because the minions' lane walk is that layer's own. The match log records the
whole variant, rule constants included, as `map`, and `--verify` replays it. `pvp-1` stays
`DEFAULT_MAP`. The pvp-1 and v1 mock match logs are byte-identical before and after this branch, and
PR #88's 20 Jev logs replay-verify on it.

**Decisions taken while building** (the approval didn't fix these):
- **Boost size and base:** ×1.5, and "in base" means within 200. On pvp-2, the respawn points are 120
  (mid) and 170 (sides) from the base, and the recall lands on it.
- **The boost ends on a hit from any source.** In base it comes back on, so a fight inside the base is
  boosted only on the ticks the bot isn't hit.
- **The teleport:**
  - damage doesn't interrupt it;
  - another action, starting a recall, death or the tower falling does;
  - the cooldown runs from the landing, so a cancelled channel costs only the time spent standing in
    it;
  - it starts ready, and it isn't in the sim's `cooldowns`, so the Metronome doesn't shorten it and a
    respawn doesn't reset it.
- **What pilots see:**
  - every teleport in progress, either team's;
  - the bot's own team's standing towers, map-wide, each with how many enemy bearbots are within 260
    of it (a tower's sight);
  - `self.speedBoost`.
- **The shop radius scales with the map** (150 → 200 on pvp-2), so it still covers every respawn
  point, as economy-spec §3.6 requires.

**Entrants and the vocabulary:**
- vocab-2 states the boost, the teleport, the team's towers and every teleport in progress, but only
  when the observation has them, so every pvp-1 description is unchanged
  (`docs/vocabulary-spec.md` §8.7).
- `compile.py --map pvp-2` offers a vocab-2 compile the teleport and its two targets: `tp_lane_tower`
  and `tp_threatened_tower`.
- Without `--map`, every prompt is byte for byte what it was.

### 0.2 The geometry

Coverage per lane, from `laneCoverage` (`node pl_geom.mjs`):
- **neutral:** the stretch between the two front towers that neither covers;
- **blank behind:** the stretch between a team's own back and front towers that neither covers.

| lane | pvp-1 length | pvp-1 neutral | pvp-1 blank behind | pvp-2 length | pvp-2 neutral | pvp-2 blank behind | base → own front tower (pvp-1 / pvp-2) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| top / bottom | 1,600 | 320 | 0 | 2,128 | 318 | 84 each side | 480 / 745 |
| mid | 1,131 | 132 | 0 | 1,505 | **56** | **4** each side | 339 / 564 |

- **Mid is short for range 160.** Between the two back towers there are 1,023 units of mid lane. The
  four towers cover 960 of them wherever they stand, so mid can never have more than 63 uncovered units
  in total. At 0.375 that is 56 neutral plus a 4-unit blank behind each front tower. Moving mid's front
  towers forward trades neutral ground for blank behind, one for one. A real mid blank needs a bigger
  scale or a shorter range.
- **Walks:** walks take 1.33× as long. A minion wave reaches mid-lane in 22 s (pvp-1: 17) and the
  side-lane middle in 31 s (pvp-1: 24).

## 1. Method (written and committed before the first paid match)

### 1.1 What is compared

- **The pvp-1 arm is PR #88's Jev block.** That is 20 matches with the same schemas, flags and seeds,
  and its logs replay-verify on this branch, so this code plays them exactly as #88 did. Jev isn't
  deterministic (PR #37), so block R re-plays six of them on pvp-1 now, as a drift check.
- **The pvp-2 arm is the same 20 matches with `--map pvp-2`.** Nothing else changes: the same house
  schemas and the same `sample-entrant-siege` schemas.
- **The house bots and the siege entrant have no teleport rule.** So the pvp-2 arm measures the map,
  the towers and the boost, and block T measures the teleport.

### 1.2 The teleport entrant

- **`prompts/pilots/sample-entrant-pvp2.prose.md`** is the siege entrant's prose plus one sentence:
  "When my teleport is ready, an enemy bearbot is within 260 units of one of my towers, and no enemy is
  in sight, I teleport to the tower they are attacking."
- **Its schema is spliced, as in #71/#79/#88.** `sample-entrant-pvp2.schemas.json` is
  `sample-entrant-siege.schemas.json` byte for byte, plus that rule's object. It is taken from one free
  compile (`compile.py --vocab vocab-2 --economy eco-3-late --map pvp-2 --backend ollama`, sample t1)
  and placed right after "will an enemy tower shoot this bot?", as the prose orders it.
- **Why splice:** t1 compiled the teleport rule correctly for all three instruments: "teleport ready
  AND an enemy bearbot within 260 units of one of this bot's towers AND no enemies in sight" →
  `ability teleport tp_threatened_tower`. But its violin siege question came out as "do that enemy
  tower's minions have this bot", the same failure #88 screened out. Splicing keeps the teleport rule
  the only difference between the two entrants.

### 1.4 The lines

The headline is line 1, the goal Ceryce named: towers fall before sudden death.

1. **Towers before 8:00 (P2-A + B + C, 18 matches).** Counted: the matches in which any tower falls
   before 480 s. The pvp-1 baseline is 0 of 18 (#88).
   - **met** iff ≥ 6 of 18 (a third);
   - **moved** iff 1–5;
   - **not moved** iff 0.

   The stand-in predicts 5 of 36 before its 1.8× overstatement, so "moved" or "not moved" is the
   expectation.
2. **First tower down, earlier (P2-A + B + C).** PASS iff the median time of the first tower down is
   earlier than the pvp-1 arm's median (#88 A + B + C: 501 s). The quartiles of both are reported.
3. **Still decisive (P2-A + B + C).** PASS iff ≥ 15 of 18 are decided. The baseline is 18 of 18.
4. **Ladder guard (P2-C + D).** PASS iff both hold:
   - hard wins ≥ 2/3 of C's decided matches;
   - easy wins none of D's.
5. **The entrant doesn't feed (P2-B).** PASS iff entrant3 dies ≤ 10 times a match against easy. The
   pvp-1 figure is 0.5.
6. **The teleport is used (T-M).** PASS iff the teleport entrant lands at least 1 teleport a match on
   average. Reported beside it, against P2-A's plain entrant on the same seeds:
   - decided, W–L;
   - first tower down;
   - the entrant's towers lost and deaths.
7. **Clean:** every match finishes and replay-verifies, with 0 server errors and 0 parse errors.
8. **Spend:** at most $5.00 in all, by the server's ledger.

**Reported, not lines:**
- match length;
- end reasons;
- towers fallen a match;
- deaths, gold spent and unspent a bot, and structure damage, per side;
- the weakest enemy tower's hp lost by 7:59;
- the boost's breaks (damage, river);
- block R against #88's C (decided, first tower down, hp lost by 7:59);
- T-H.

### 1.5 The teleport cooldown (one constant)

The approval says start at 90 s, and use 60 s "if matches lengthen to ~13 min". The rule, fixed here:
**60 s iff the median match length over all pvp-2 matches in this run is ≥ 750 s (12.5 min); otherwise
90 s.**
- The frozen sim ends every match by 600 s (`MATCH_DURATION_SEC`), and the Final Chorus starts sudden
  death at 480 s. Under today's clock a pvp-2 match cannot reach 13 minutes, so this run keeps 90 s
  unless the clock is changed.
- The match length is still measured, and so is how often pvp-2 matches run to the 10:00 cap, which is
  the sign that a longer clock (and then 60 s) is worth deciding.

### 1.6 Amendment: one follow-up block, T-M2 (committed after §1's results were seen, before its own first paid match)

This amendment is post hoc. §1's 36 matches were played and read before it was written, and none of
their lines change because of it.

- **Why.** Line 6 failed on Jev: the teleport entrant started 29.7 channels a match against medium and
  landed 0.33. The cause is a description bug of this branch's own, not Jev:
  - While a bot channels, vocab-2 said "Its teleport is channelling to its tower …" and no longer said
    it was ready.
  - The rule's question begins "is this bot's teleport ready?". With the same facts mid-channel, Jev
    answered yes 6 % of the time (6 of 100), against 99 % (189 of 191) when the line said ready.
  - So at the next decision (2 s, the median life of a channel) a lower rule fired, and the channel
    was cancelled.
- **The fix** (`fidelity_harness._map_rule_lines`): while it channels, the line says "Its teleport is
  ready and in use: it is channelling to its tower tw-7, landing in 3 s, and choosing any other action
  cancels it." Nothing else changes.
- **The block:** T-M2, a re-play of T-M (entrantTP–medium and medium–entrantTP, seeds 3, 7, 11, pvp-2,
  the §1.3 lines) at the fix's commit.
- **Backend and spend.** A fresh private server on `:8963` with `--budget-usd 0.90`, and a runner stop
  at $0.85. §1 spent $4.0117 by its server's ledger, so the job stays under $5.00 in all. Never
  extended.
- **Line 6′.** PASS iff the teleport entrant lands at least 1 teleport a match on average. Reported
  beside it against T-M and P2-A:
  - decided, W–L;
  - first tower down;
  - towers lost;
  - deaths;
  - why channels end.
