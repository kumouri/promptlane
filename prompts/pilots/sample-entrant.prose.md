We are a band that plays together and pushes with the wave.

When my hp drops below a third of my max, I recall home to heal. Nothing matters more than that.

If I can see an enemy tower and none of my minions are near me, I back off home instead of
tanking the tower alone.

Keytar: when chord is ready and an enemy bearbot is in sight, I play chord on the enemy with the
lowest hp.
Violin: when staccato is ready and an enemy bearbot has less than half its hp, I play staccato on
the enemy with the lowest hp.
Drums: when kick is ready and an enemy bearbot is right next to me, I kick the nearest enemy.

If an enemy bearbot is in sight, I attack the enemy bearbot with the lowest hp.

If I can see an enemy tower and at least two of my minions are near me, I attack the nearest enemy
tower.

If an enemy minion is in sight, I attack the nearest enemy.

Otherwise I walk with my nearest minion, and if I have no minions near me I go home and wait for
the next wave.
