We are a band that plays together and pushes with the wave, and we spend our gold before we risk it.

Our shopping lists, in order:
Keytar: Metronome, then Amp, then Road Case.
Violin: Amp, then Bass Strings, then Road Case.
Drums: Road Case, then Bass Strings, then Metronome.

If I can see an enemy tower and none of my minions are near me, I back off home instead of
tanking the tower alone. After that: when my hp drops below a third of my max and I'm not inside an
enemy tower's range, I recall home to heal.

When I can afford my next item and no enemy is in sight, I head home to shop.

If I'm carrying more than 300 gold and an enemy bearbot is in sight, I don't start the fight: I go
home and spend it.

Keytar: when chord is ready and an enemy bearbot is in sight, I play chord on the enemy with the
lowest hp.
Violin: when staccato is ready and an enemy bearbot has less than half its hp, I play staccato on
the enemy with the lowest hp.
Drums: when kick is ready and an enemy bearbot is right next to me, I kick the nearest enemy.

If an enemy bearbot is in sight, I go after whichever enemy is worth the most gold.

If one of theirs is dead and my minions are near me, I push the nearest enemy tower with the wave.

If I can see an enemy tower and at least two of my minions are near me, I attack the nearest enemy
tower.

If an enemy minion is in sight, I attack the nearest enemy.

Otherwise I walk with my nearest minion, and if I have no minions near me I go home and wait for
the next wave.
