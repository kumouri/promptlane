We are a band that takes towers. We walk with our wave, hit their tower while our minions take its
shots, step out when it turns on us, and spend our gold before we risk it.

Our shopping lists, in order:
Keytar: Metronome, then Amp, then Road Case, then Click Track.
Violin: Amp, then Bass Strings, then Road Case, then Fuzz Pedal.
Drums: Road Case, then Bass Strings, then Amp, then Backline.

When my hp is below half of my max and an enemy minion, enemy tower or enemy bearbot is in sight, I
walk back home. When my hp is below half of my max and no enemy is in sight, I recall home.

When I can afford my next item and no enemy is in sight, I recall home to shop.

At eight minutes every tower drops to a third of its hp and the first tower to fall wins, so when it
is more than 480 seconds into the match and I can see an enemy tower, I attack the nearest enemy
tower.

When an enemy tower will shoot me, I fall back to my own tower.

Destroying the enemy base tower wins the match, and it can be hit once one of their inner towers
is down, so when the enemy base tower can be hit, I attack the enemy base tower.

When I am inside an enemy tower's range and that tower has my own minions in its range to shoot
first, I attack the nearest enemy tower.

When an enemy bearbot is in sight, I attack the nearest enemy bearbot.

When an enemy minion is in sight, I attack the nearest enemy minion.

When one of my minions is near me, I walk with my nearest minion.

Otherwise I push down my lane toward the enemy base.
